## key lesson learned

**context before content.** 

when sharing work in communities:
1. explain what you're building (1-2 sentences)
2. explain why it matters to the community
3. explain what you're asking for (feedback? collaboration? just sharing?)
4. **then** share the link

this respects readers' time and attention. they can decide relevance **before** clicking, not after.

does this make sense? it's the difference between:
- ❌ "check out my repo! [link]"
- ✅ "i'm building a risc-v vere fork for urbit. here's the design doc: [link]. thoughts on the nock execution approach?"
